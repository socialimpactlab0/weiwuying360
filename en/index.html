DISCOVER WEIWUYING

Weiwuying is a destination in its own right

If you arrive only minutes before the curtain rises, you may miss much of what makes this place special—from its architecture and open spaces to the greenery right outside.

01

World-class architecture worth seeing

Its flowing roof, open entrances and park setting connect seamlessly. Weiwuying was also selected for TIME's “World's Greatest Places 2019.”

02

Banyan Plaza brings the theater into everyday life

This semi-open public space invites you to stroll, pause and experience the scale, light and atmosphere of the building before entering the auditorium.

03

Weiwuying Metropolitan Park is right next door

Lawns, mature trees and walking paths make it easy to arrive early, slow down and enjoy the surroundings before the performance.

Turn “going to a show” into “spending a beautiful afternoon.”
When you slow down before entering the theater, the performance can feel even more special.

When a world-class theater meets Shen Yun

Classical Chinese dance, live orchestra, exquisite costumes and animated backdrops come together as a complete stage experience. If it is your first time, Weiwuying is a memorable place to begin. If you have seen Shen Yun before, a different theater offers a new way to experience it.
