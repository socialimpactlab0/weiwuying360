A BEAUTIFUL AFTERNOON

Before Shen Yun, make Weiwuying part of the experience

There is no need to over-plan. Enjoy the architecture, have coffee, walk through the greenery, and let the afternoon slow down before you enter the Opera House.

Have a coffeeGOR’ Coffee · Weiwuying 2F
Take a walkWeiwuying Metropolitan Park
Add one more stopHuangpu New Village
It is not “arrive two hours early.” It is “enjoy two extra hours.”Once the performance is surrounded by scenery, coffee and a relaxed walk, the whole day becomes more memorable.
CHOOSE YOUR STYLE

How would you like to spend the day?

You do not need to do everything. Pick one route that matches your pace.

Best for first-time visitors, families and an easy pace
About 1.5–2 hours

Coffee → Banyan Plaza → Park → Performance

Stay within Weiwuying for the most relaxed option and the least risk of rushing.

1
Sit down at GOR’ CoffeeChoose the 2F public-piano area or the 3F Crown Hall location for coffee, sweets or a light meal.
2
Explore Banyan PlazaTake in the building's signature curves, scale and open public space.
3
Walk the park, then returnLeave enough time to find your hall, use the restroom and settle in.
Best for old houses, photography and café stops
About 2.5–3.5 hours

Huangpu Village → Café → Weiwuying → Shen Yun

The preserved military-village houses and greenery create a slower, more local side of Fengshan.

1
Walk through Huangpu New VillageExplore old houses, shady lanes and the traces of everyday village life.
2
Choose a café in the villageExamples include Donkey Lab and TO DAY COFFEE; check same-day opening hours.
3
Return to WeiwuyingPlan to be back at least 45–60 minutes before curtain time.
Best for visitors who want one more architectural stop
About 3–4 hours

Dadong → Local Fengshan food → Weiwuying → Shen Yun

Dadong's membrane canopy is a strong visual landmark, and the surrounding district makes it easy to add local food.

1
Explore Dadong Arts CenterThe semi-open plaza, canopy and changing light are worth photographing.
2
Try something localFengcheng rice cake and Liang Bo lamb are among nearby local options; check opening hours before going.
3
Take the MRT back to WeiwuyingDadong and Weiwuying are both on the Orange Line, keeping the transfer simple.
